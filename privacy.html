DailyKit Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

DailyKit ("the app") is a free Android app with everyday tools: habit tracking, a water tracker, a budget tracker, a bill splitter, a tip calculator and a unit converter. This policy explains what information the app uses.

Information you enter

Everything you enter, such as habits, water intake, budget amounts, and expense names, is saved only on your device. We do not have accounts, we do not collect this data, and we cannot see it. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use certain data to show and measure ads, including your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and approximate location. This data is handled by Google under its own policies. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your Android settings under Settings > Google > Ads.

Information we do not collect

  • We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or contacts.
  • We do not access your camera, microphone, photos or precise location.
  • We do not sell personal information.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 13 we will take steps to delete that information.

Backups & Retention

The app stores data on the device only. If you enable Android automatic backups on your device, app data may be included in device backups managed by Google. Uninstalling the app removes data stored locally on the device.

Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data stored by the app on your device, but no method of storage is completely secure.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy: https://dailykit.app/contact
